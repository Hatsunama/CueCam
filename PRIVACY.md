# CueCam Privacy Policy

Effective August 13, 2026

CueCam does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data. The app has no account system, analytics, advertising, or backend service.

Scripts and prompt preferences are stored locally on the device. Android cloud backup is disabled. Uninstalling CueCam removes its local app data, subject to the phone operating system's normal behavior.

CueCam uses camera and microphone access only to record videos initiated by the user. It requests add-only media-library access to save completed clips to the phone's normal gallery. CueCam does not browse or upload existing photos or videos.

Questions about this policy can be submitted through the CueCam repository at https://github.com/Hatsunama/CueCam/issues.
