# CueCam Privacy Policy

Effective September 22, 2026

CueCam does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data. The app has no accounts, analytics, advertising, or backend service.

Scripts and prompt preferences are stored locally on the device. Android cloud backup is disabled.

CueCam uses camera and microphone access only to record videos initiated by the user. It requests add-only media-library access to save completed clips to the phone's normal gallery. CueCam does not browse or upload existing photos or videos.

## Account and data deletion

CueCam has no account to delete. Uninstalling CueCam removes its local app data, subject to the phone operating system. Videos already saved to the phone gallery remain there until the user deletes them in the gallery app.
